Frequently Asked Questions

What information do I need to provide when booking?

You will need to provide your full name, contact information, pickup date and return date, and the car model you wish to rent when booking a vehicle.

What information do I need to provide at time of pickup?

At the time of pickup, you will need to present a valid driver's license to verify that you are eligible to rent a vehicle and drive.

How can I modify, make changes or cancel a reservation?

You can modify or cancel your reservation up to 24 hours before the pickup date for a full refund. Any changes or cancellations made within 24 hours of the pickup date will not be given a refund.

Should I return the vehicle with a full tank of gas?

Yes, you are required to return the vehicle with a full tank of gas. If you don't, you will be charged for the cost of the missing fuel.
